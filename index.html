Methodology and limitations
  • Entry: buy when the close crosses above the 15-period SMA.
  • Exit: sell when the price drops below the SMA, or force-close at the final data point.
  • Constraints: max 8 trades, 0.1% fee per side, 0 BTC held at termination.
  • Fee friction: each round trip costs about 0.2%, so the strategy needs more than that in gross gains per trade to break even.
  • Forced liquidation: closing flat at the end removes any chance of mean-reversion recovery.
  • Lag: moving-average signals trail price and produce false signals in range-bound markets.
